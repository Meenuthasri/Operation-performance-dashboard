# Operation Performance Analyst – Business Insight Report

## 1. Executive Summary

The **Operation Performance Analyst Dashboard** provides management with a consolidated view of:

- Operational workload
- Productivity
- Turnaround Time (TAT)
- Chargeable activity
- Audit performance
- Training status
- Employee-level quality performance

The dashboard helps management answer key business questions:

- Are operational teams processing work efficiently?
- Is the organization keeping up with incoming workload?
- How is operational performance changing month over month?
- What is the current turnaround-time position?
- How many cases are being audited?
- How many audited cases contain errors?
- Which employees require additional quality or performance attention?
- What proportion of cases are related to training?
- Are there operational bottlenecks requiring management intervention?

> **Management Perspective:** Overall processing capacity is broadly aligned with incoming workload. However, audit errors and the displayed negative TAT require management attention.

5. Average TAT – -1.157792614
Business Insight

The dashboard displays an Average TAT of -1.157792614.

A negative TAT is generally not meaningful from a normal operational turnaround-time perspective because TAT should normally represent the elapsed time between assignment and completion.

Important Management Observation

This KPI should be validated before being used as an operational performance metric.

A negative TAT may indicate:

Incorrect date calculation
Assignment date occurring after completion date
Incorrect date/time conversion
Data-quality issues
Incorrect TAT formula
Time-zone/date-definition issues
Reversed start and end dates
Recommended Business Rule

A typical TAT calculation should follow:

TAT = Complete Date - Assign Date

Management should investigate records producing negative TAT values before using this KPI for employee or operational performance evaluation.

Priority: High – Incorrect TAT calculations can lead to incorrect conclusions about operational efficiency.

15. Key Management Findings
15.1 Processing Capacity is Stable

Processed volume is 48,297, compared with 48,290 received, resulting in a positive difference of 7 cases.

This indicates that processing capacity is broadly keeping pace with incoming workload.

15.2 Chargeability is Reasonably Strong

Average chargeability is 73%, indicating that a substantial portion of operational capacity is being used for chargeable work.

Management should continue monitoring productive utilization while understanding the business purpose of non-chargeable activities.

15.3 Quality Requires Attention

There are 184 audit errors across 1,834 audited cases, representing approximately a 10% audit error rate.

This should be investigated to identify root causes and training requirements.

15.4 September Shows Higher Operational Activity

The monthly trend indicates a significant increase in processing activity around September.

Management should understand the reason for this peak to improve future workforce and capacity planning.

15.5 Training is Concentrated in One Account

Dream Insurance contributes the majority of training-related cases.

Account size

Actual performance gaps
15.6 TAT Requires Data Validation

18. Management Conclusion

The Operation Performance Analyst Dashboard provides a useful operational control framework by combining:

Workload
Productivity
Chargeability
Turnaround Time
Audit Quality
Training
Employee-Level Performance

into a single management view.

The strongest positive indicator is that processed volume is slightly higher than received volume, suggesting that the operation is currently keeping pace with workload.

However, the approximately 10% audit error rate indicates an opportunity to improve quality through targeted root-cause analysis and employee/process training.

The negative Average TAT is the most important data-quality issue identified in the dashboard and should be validated before management uses TAT as a performance indicator.

19. Overall Management Message

Operational throughput is broadly stable, but management should focus on reducing audit errors, validating TAT calculations, optimizing chargeable utilization, and using employee-level error-rate analysis to drive targeted performance improvement.
